# Warcraft RPG 2e conversion ledger

Implementation choices stay explicit when source tables and prose disagree. PDF page numbers refer to `World_of_Warcraft_2nd_Edition.pdf`; printed pages are two lower.

| Topic | Conflicting sources | Current policy |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Night elf ability adjustment | Race summary, PDF 35: +2 Spirit, -2 Intellect. Detailed traits, PDF 41: +2 Spirit, -2 Charisma. | Unresolved. Do not import an automated penalty until campaign ruling. |
| Hunter class skills | Class-skill table, PDF 116, omits Hunter. Hunter class text, PDF 76, supplies class skills. | Use Hunter class text when Hunter enters the content slice. |
| Paladin aura name | Table, PDF 81: Spirit Aura. Prose, PDF 80: Aura of Wisdom. | Treat as one feature; display name remains unresolved until Paladin import. |
| Rogue Trap Sense at level 3 | Rogue prose, PDF 83, grants Trap Sense +1 at level 3. The class table on PDF 84 first prints “+2” at level 6 and leaves level 3 blank. | Follow the prose and the evident +1/+2/+3 progression: grant Trap Sense +1 at level 3 and advance it every three levels. |
| Tinker Knowledge skills | Tinker text and starting package, PDFs 88–90, call for broad Knowledge access and name engineering, geography, and history. The consolidated Warcraft skill table on PDF 116 does not contain those skills. | Keep the final consolidated Warcraft skill schema. Enable the listed Knowledge categories that exist in that schema; record the unavailable categories here until an Ultra-mode schema ruling. |

| Arcanite heavy-armor cost | Special-material table, PDF 195 / printed p. 193, prints the heavy-armor modifier as “+18,0000 gp,” unlike the consistent 6,000/12,000/18,000 progression. | Treat the extra zero as a typographical error and record the heavy-armor modifier as +18,000 gp. |

| Orc racial levels | Racial-level overview, PDF 35 / printed p. 33, explicitly says orc heroes may start with an orc racial level. The detailed Orc traits on PDF 48 / printed p. 46 omit the usual “Racial Levels” entry and the book contains no Orc class table or progression. | Mark Orc racial levels as source-supported but unavailable for progression until another authoritative Warcraft 2e source supplies the missing table. Do not invent Hit Dice, saves, or features. |
| Legacy Hide + Move Silently conversion | Warcraft replaces both skills with Stealth, but existing D35E worlds may have paid ranks in each. Adding the investments would exceed the normal rank ceiling; discarding one without a record would lose data. | Migration 3.1.3 backs up both skill objects, uses the greater stored investment for Stealth, and records the lower investment as potentially refundable points for GM review. |
| Chilled action restriction | Chilled, PDF 247 / printed p. 245, permits either a move or standard action, but not both or a full-round action. Foundry’s core action workflow does not provide a universal action-spending gate. | Automate the numeric -2 AC/melee attack/melee damage/Reflex and halved Jump effects. Present Chilled as a condition; the one-action restriction remains GM-enforced. |

| Situational racial effects and choices | Several racial traits depend on target identity, terrain, time/light state, an unstructured weapon choice, or a daily choice of eligible spells or feats. Foundry has no reliable universal context signal for these cases. | Automate unconditional numerical effects, uses, durations, healing, and progression. Keep target/terrain/light checks and player choices visible on the race or granted feature for GM adjudication rather than applying a bonus to invalid rolls. |
| Forsaken revival | Forsaken traits, PDF 54-55 / printed pp. 52-53, reject *raise dead* and *reincarnate*, permit *resurrection* and *true resurrection* to restore undead existence, and require *wish* or *miracle* to restore the former living creature. | Preserve the rule in the Forsaken trait record. Spell targeting remains manual until the resurrection workflow can express alternate returned creature states without destructive actor replacement. |
| Universal action restrictions | Disabled and Chilled states restrict actions, while the inherited system exposes many independent item, macro, and module-driven action paths. | Apply the printed numerical penalties and present explicit condition reminders. The GM enforces one-move-or-standard limits and the disabled living creature's 1 HP strenuous-action loss; a partial gate would be less safe than a documented manual boundary. |
| Generic undead recovery wording | D&D 3.5 SRD `monsterTypes.html#undead` says unintelligent undead cannot heal damage on their own, while intelligent undead are not given a standard natural-rest recovery rate. Warcraft Monster Guide undead inherit undead traits. | Use a conservative generic Warcraft-undead profile: destroyed at 0 HP, no ordinary-rest HP recovery, negative energy healing, Charisma for Concentration, and SRD undead immunities. Fast healing remains effective. Forsaken keep their separate -10 destruction rule. |
| Lady Onyxia caster level | Monster Guide printed p. 164 gives an explicit Mage Spells block with caster level 19. The Magical Nature prose on printed p. 165 instead says her dragon and mage levels make her a 21st-level mage. | Use caster level 19 for Onyxia's executable spellbook because it is the direct value in the complete spellcasting block. Preserve the level-21 conflict here rather than silently recalculating her slots or caster level. |
| Nefarian caster levels | Monster Guide printed p. 172 gives an explicit Warlock Spells block with caster level 27, then gives caster level 31 for Conjuration (summoning). Magical Nature on the same page instead says the base total is caster level 30. | Use caster level 27 for Nefarian's general executable spellbook. Keep summoning at caster level 31 when that exception is adjudicated; do not apply the conflicting level-30 prose globally. |

Every imported Warcraft document should keep a short provenance note. Mechanics may be paraphrased; book artwork and wholesale text must remain outside distributable system files.
